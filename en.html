About

French craft… baked fresh every day in Zamalek

At 13 Brazil Street in Mohamed Mazhar, Zamalek, Pain des Rois serves artisan French viennoiseries and coffee in a calm, Parisian setting, since 2022. From loaded croissants to baguette and classic pâtisserie — everything is made with care so it reaches you fresh.

  • An artisan French bakery & café — Viennoiseries & Pâtisseries / Café
  • Loaded croissants, flaky and multi-layered, baked fresh daily
  • French pâtisserie & breakfast and carefully chosen coffee, in a Parisian atmosphere
